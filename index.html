I build compound AI and multi-agent systems that model customer behavior across search, selection, and econometrics at Amazon Stores Science. My research spans LLM-agent reasoning, agent reward modeling, RL from AI feedback, knowledge-graph reasoning, and graph–language model integration, with work published at ICLR, NeurIPS, KDD, WWW, WSDM, IJCAI and one US patent. Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at Virginia Tech with Dr. Chandan Reddy, and my Masters & Bachelors at IIIT, Hyderabad.

Research Focus

  Compound & Multi-Agent AI

Optimizing LLM-agent pipelines via globally-aligned local rewards (Optimas, ICLR 2026), LLM-agent recommendation with implicit item relations (AgentDR, WWW 2026), and RL from AI feedback (RecLAIF).

  LLMs + Knowledge Graphs

Multi-modal LLM encoders for graph-structured data (GT2Vec), complex logical reasoning over KGs (arXiv 2023), and graph–language ensembles for search relevance (WWW 2024).

  Geometric Representation Learning

Scalable hyperbolic GNNs via meta-learning (NeurIPS 2023), unification of Euclidean & hyperbolic GNNs (IJCAI 2023), and probabilistic entity reasoning (NeurIPS 2021).
