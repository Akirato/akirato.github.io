Research

Research Vision

I work on the foundations and applications of compound AI and multi-agent systems — LLM-driven pipelines whose components retrieve, reason, plan, and act in coordination. At Amazon Stores Science, this translates to multi-agent customer-behavior models that jointly optimize search, selection, and econometrics. Three threads run through my research:

  1. Optimization & reward design for compound AI. How do we train and align multi-component LLM systems when end-to-end signals are sparse and credit assignment is hard? Recent work (Optimas, ICLR 2026; AgentDR, WWW 2026; RecLAIF) introduces globally-aligned local rewards, LLM-agent recommendation with implicit relations, and reinforcement learning from AI feedback for agentic pipelines.
  2. Reasoning over structured knowledge with LLMs. Bridging neural-symbolic reasoning and foundation models for retrieval, ranking, and complex logical queries (WWW 2024, ArXiv 2023, GT2Vec).
  3. Geometric representation learning at scale. Hyperbolic and non-Euclidean networks for hierarchical data, deployed in production search (NeurIPS 2023, WSDM 2022, NeurIPS 2021).

My work has shipped to Amazon Stores, appeared at ICLR / NeurIPS / KDD / WWW / WSDM / IJCAI, and is reflected in one US patent and several open-source releases under amazon-science.
